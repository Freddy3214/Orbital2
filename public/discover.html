PRESS & COMMUNITY

A small game. A wide frontier.

Orbital Foundry is a free, AI-assisted browser space-strategy alpha. It combines colony building, research, cargo missions, scouting and timed fleet attacks. It is seeking early testers, not claiming a completed large-scale MMO.
